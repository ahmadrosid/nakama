Writing about Nakama, adding it to an integration list, or putting it on a slide? Use the files on this page so the logo looks the same everywhere.

The mark is the **Trio**: three people, linked. *Nakama* means crew, the friends you work beside, and Nakama agents work with your team, not instead of it.

Use SVG when you can. It stays sharp at every size. The PNG logos are 1200 px wide with a transparent background.

## Logo

Use the logo wherever there is room for the name: docs, READMEs, sign-in pages, social cards, and partner pages.

![Nakama logo, black on a light background](/brand/preview-logo-light.png)

On dark backgrounds, use the white version.

![Nakama logo, white on a dark background](/brand/preview-logo-dark.png)

## Mark

Use the mark alone when space is tight or the name is already nearby: favicons, avatars, app icons, and small buttons.

![Nakama mark, white on dark](/brand/preview-mark-dark.png)

## App icon

The app icon is the white mark on a near-black tile. Use it for download links, app directories, and anywhere other apps show their icon.

![Nakama app icon](/brand/nakama-app-icon-512.png)

## Clear space

Keep empty space around the logo equal to the width of one dot in the mark. Do not put text, borders, or other logos inside it.

![Clear space around the Nakama logo is one dot width on every side](/brand/preview-clear-space.png)

## Minimum size

| | Smallest size |
|---|---|
| Mark | 16 px tall |
| Logo | 16 px tall |

Below 16 px the wordmark gets hard to read. Use the mark alone there.

![The Nakama mark and logo at real pixel sizes](/brand/preview-sizes.png)

## Colours

The logo is one colour: ink on light backgrounds, white on dark ones. Amber is the product's accent colour, for highlights next to the logo, not inside it.

![Nakama colours: ink, white, amber for light themes, amber for dark themes](/brand/preview-colours.png)

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#0A0A0A` | Logo on light backgrounds, app icon tile |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Logo on dark backgrounds, mark on the app icon |
| Amber, light | `#B45309` | Accent on light themes |
| Amber, dark | `#F59E0B` | Accent on dark themes |

## Typeface

The wordmark is a lowercase **nakama** set in [Satoshi](https://www.fontshare.com/fonts/satoshi) at weight 600, with letter spacing at −3%. Use the logo files instead of typing the name in Satoshi, so the spacing and the mark stay right.

When you write the name in text, write **Nakama** with a capital N.

## Do and don't

**Do**

- Use the files from this page as they are.
- Put the black logo on light backgrounds and the white logo on dark ones.
- Leave the clear space around it.

**Don't**

- Rotate, stretch, or redraw the mark.
- Fill the triangle between the dots, or draw the dots as outlines.
- Give the dots different colours, or add gradients, shadows, or glows.
- Put the logo on busy photos or patterns.
- Change the wordmark font or write it in capitals.
