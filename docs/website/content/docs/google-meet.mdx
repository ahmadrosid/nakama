Nakama can listen to a Google Meet call through Chrome and turn the conversation into text you and your agent can use.

## Why use Google Meet capture

The Chrome extension picks up sound from the Meet tab and your microphone on your computer. Nakama sends that sound to OpenAI, which turns it into text and saves it with the meeting.

There is no bot participant joining the call.

## What you need

- An organization admin who can configure Google Meet
- An OpenAI provider in **AI Providers**. Google Meet uses its key to turn speech into text.
- Google Chrome with the Nakama extension installed
- A Google Meet call that you are allowed to record and transcribe

Always tell participants before transcription starts. Follow your organization's recording and privacy rules.

## Set up Google Meet

### 1. Open Google Meet

Open **Settings → Workspace → Google Meet**. It is included in Nakama. An organization admin can enable or disable it in **Google Meet → Settings**.

### 2. Add an OpenAI provider

Open **Settings → Agent tools → AI Providers** and add an OpenAI provider with an API key. Google Meet uses the default provider when it is OpenAI, otherwise the first OpenAI provider. OpenAI bills this usage separately from a ChatGPT subscription.

### 3. Install the Chrome extension

Download the extension from the **Google Meet** page and unzip it. This is a one-time setup in Chrome:

1. Open `chrome://extensions`
2. Turn on **Developer mode**
3. Choose **Load unpacked**
4. Select the extension folder

Keep the extension enabled while you use Google Meet.

### 4. Connect the extension to Nakama

You do this once per browser.

1. Click the Nakama icon in the Chrome toolbar. The extension opens in the side panel.
2. Enter your Nakama address, for example `https://nakama.example.com`, and choose **Connect**. Allow access to that address when Chrome asks.
3. Nakama opens in a new tab and shows a code. Check that it matches the code in the side panel, then choose **Approve**.

The side panel then shows **Connected**. Your browser appears under **Connected browsers** on the **Google Meet** page, where you can remove it. A connection lasts 90 days and renews each time you use it.

## Start a transcription

1. Join your Google Meet call in Chrome.
2. On the Meet tab, click the Nakama icon in the Chrome toolbar. Chrome lets the extension record a tab only after this click on that tab.
3. Choose **Start transcription** in the side panel.

You do not need to keep Nakama open. The side panel shows a timer and the live transcript.

The meeting moves through **Waiting**, **Transcribing**, and **Finished**. Open the meeting in Nakama to see new text while the call is running.

## Read the transcript with your agent

The agent can:

- list meetings
- check a meeting's status
- read new transcript text
- stop an active capture

Ask questions such as:

> What decisions were made in the latest Google Meet?

> Summarize the action items from meeting ABC.

Assign the Google Meet tools and skill to the profile you want to use. The tools are read-only: the agent lists meetings and reads transcripts. It sees meetings owned by that profile, plus your own captures and imports from the Google Meet page. It never sees another member's page meetings.

You can also upload a Markdown transcript (up to 1 MiB) or audio file (up to 7 MiB). Audio uploads use the organization's regular transcription settings; live capture uses the OpenAI provider from **AI Providers**.

## Import a recorded meeting

If a Meet call was recorded, open **Google Meet** and choose **Import recording**. The first time, the dialog lists **Gmail** and **Google Drive**. Choose **Connect** next to each one and sign in with Google. Nakama turns on the integration for your organization and brings you back to Google Meet. Recording import needs Composio: if it is not set up, ask your platform admin. It also needs an OpenAI provider in **AI Providers**.

Select the video from your Meet recording email and choose **Import**. Keep the page open while Nakama downloads the video and sends its audio to OpenAI Whisper. The finished transcript appears in meeting history.

The recording must be no larger than 1 GiB or two hours. If an import fails, choose it again. Imported transcripts do not identify speakers.

## Stop and save

Choose **Stop** in Nakama or **Stop transcription** in the side panel. Nakama finishes the current transcript and keeps the saved meeting available under **Saved transcripts**.

If Chrome closes or the connection breaks, Nakama keeps the text collected so far and marks the meeting as incomplete. It does not silently reconnect to the call.

## Nakama behind a reverse proxy

The extension sends audio to the same address you connected it to. If Nakama runs behind a reverse proxy, the proxy must forward WebSocket upgrade requests for `/v1/meet/capture`. Use `https://` for any address outside your own computer.

## Troubleshooting

### The capture does not start

- Click the Nakama icon in the toolbar on the Meet tab, then choose **Start transcription** again.
- Make sure the extension is enabled.
- If the side panel shows **Not connected**, connect it again. Nakama disconnects a browser when you remove it, change your password, or leave the organization.

### The meeting is ready but no transcript appears

- Check that an OpenAI provider with an API key exists in **AI Providers**.
- Check that Google Meet is enabled in **Google Meet → Settings**. Capture starts automatically; there is no separate worker to start.
- Confirm that Chrome allowed microphone access.
- Confirm that participants are speaking in the Meet tab being captured.

### The extension cannot reach Nakama

Check the address in the side panel settings. For a remote server, ask your administrator to check the secure connection, the firewall, and WebSocket forwarding for `/v1/meet/capture`.
